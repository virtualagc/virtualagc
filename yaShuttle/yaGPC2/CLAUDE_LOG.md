### [2026-10-16] Target: RENDEZVOUS_PLAN.md (RPOP section)
- RPOP predictors: rpop.py now has --predictor hold|coast (default hold, a straight line along the relative velocity; coast = Clohessy-Wiltshire free drift, exact against two-body motion). Owner (2026-10-16): hold is more useful for guidance but a straight line is known to be wrong for a sophisticated user, and coast is probably wrong too -- the real RPOP's predictor rule is not public. JSC-63400 Fig 25.31 (p249, receding at 0.34 ft/s) digits curve UP, fitting CW within ~8 px at 45 s spacing (x spacing fits 55-65 s; pixel readings from a recreation). Figs 25.14/25.19/25.32 do not discriminate; 25.23 (p243) has no predictors, its state is corrupted (Xdot -5.13) so CW marks would fall off-window. Open: the real increment (45-65 s?) and whether the real predictors are CW. TORVA goal puts the CG on the V-bar, so the flip to docking attitude leaves the ring ~63 ft off the axis (Z -63 at 277 ft): fix candidate is aiming the ring.
