- THE .000001 LITERAL IS DIAGNOSED, and it is not what issue #1296 supposes. It is not a source-scanning bug and not syntax-dependent at all. Ten spellings were tested and every one converts CORRECTLY to A0B5ED8D: `0.000001 SCALAR$(@DOUBLE)(C)`, the same with the operands reversed, `1.0E-6`, `.000001`, a bare `0.000001 C`, and all of those again under `X + ...`, `X - ...` and unary minus. Exponent and decimal-fraction notation converge exactly, and the pool does not deduplicate, so it is not a merge artefact either.
- The literal is NOT IN GO8ORB'S SOURCE, nor anywhere in its include closure. It arrives through a TEMPLATE. Each of the four affected units includes an *_HFE PROGRAM template that has the literal in ITS source: GO8ORB and GO2ORB include GEI_ORBIT_HFE (GEIORB.hal:180, `SCALAR$(@DOUBLE)(CZ1V_HFE_TFCMLTQH) 1.0E-6`); GO1ASC includes GEA_ASC_RTLS_HFE; GO3ENT includes GEP_ENTRY_HFE. The SDFs for all three of those — ##GEIORB, ##GEAASC, ##GEPENT — hold the CORRECT A0B5ED8D.
- So a value stored correctly in a template's SDF comes out of the template-inclusion path as A0B5ED8E. The corruption is on the SDF-constant to literal-pool path, not in scanning source text. That is why exactly 18 units that compile the literal from their own source are right and exactly 4 that inherit it through a template are wrong.
- The value is NOT in ##GEIORB's initializationTable (76 entries, checked). It sits at byte offset 0x76A8, consistent with a constant-value or HALMAT cell — modules/sdf has parseConstantValueCell and parseHalmatCellChain for those.
